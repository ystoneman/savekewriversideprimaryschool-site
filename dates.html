From Richmond Council

Dates, leaflet & FAQs

Richmond Council proposes closing Kew Riverside Primary School at the end of the 2026/27 school year, from 1 September 2027. No final decision has been made.

Response deadline

The council’s response form says: “All responses must be submitted by 16 October 2026.” No closing time is given, and we have not seen an extension. The leaflet’s table below gives this stage as September/November 2026. Respond to the council ↗︎

The published response deadline, 16 October 2026, has passed. No final decision has been made. The council’s timetable below shows the planned next stages.

The council’s documents and FAQs

  • Consultation leaflet (PDF, 8 pages) The council’s Kew proposal, its reasons, the alternatives it rejected, the timetable below and how to respond.
  • Kew Riverside FAQ (PDF, 13 pages) The council’s answers to points raised about Kew during this consultation, including school places and transition for current pupils if the closure goes ahead.
  • School organisation consultation FAQ (PDF, 14 pages) The council’s general answers on proposed school closures and amalgamations across Richmond; not specific to Kew.
  • Council consultation page ↗︎ The council’s current information and response route.
