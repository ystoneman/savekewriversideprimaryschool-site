6 October rally: archived details

The advertised date has passed. This page retains the planned details for reference. It does not confirm attendance or outcomes.

Tuesday 6 October 2026
6–6.45pm · Outside York House, Twickenham

Advertised schedule

The parent rally flyer advertised a peaceful gathering at York House, Richmond Road, Twickenham TW1 3AA: 6pm gathering, an optional group press photo at 6.15pm, and a 6.45pm finish.

Full Council was listed for 7pm, with York House Council Chamber as the proposed venue. That listing does not establish that the school proposal was discussed or decided.

Venue map (past event) · Original calendar file (past event)

The map locates York House, not a confirmed assembly point. The original calendar remains tentative and records the advertised time.

Original access notes

At the last pre-event update, the exact gathering spot, use of the grounds, step-free route, accessible gathering area and toilet availability were awaiting confirmation. The advertised gathering was stationary, with no march. These are historical notes, not current travel advice.

Photos remain your choice

The advertised group press photo was optional. Attending the rally does not give permission to publish someone’s image.

Ask before taking or sharing an identifiable photo of another person. For children, get their parent or carer’s agreement and respect the child’s own willingness. Independent press or other attendees may have taken photographs; organisers cannot promise that everyone stayed out of all images.

Make your view part of the consultation

Coming to the rally does not submit a consultation response. Respond to the council by 16 October 2026 in your own words; you can respond now.
