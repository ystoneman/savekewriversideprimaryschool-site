Rally at York House

Tuesday 6 October 2026
6–6.45pm · Outside York House, Twickenham

A peaceful parent-led gathering in support of keeping Kew Riverside Primary School open. Parents, carers and supporters are welcome; taking part is optional.

Check before travelling. The exact gathering spot, use of the grounds and access arrangements are awaiting confirmation. We will update this page when those details are settled. For an access question or the latest arrangements, contact Yann.

York House
Richmond Road, Twickenham TW1 3AA

The plan for Tuesday

A short gathering before Council

  1. 6pm

    Gather outside York House

    Check the confirmed meeting spot here before travelling.

  2. 6.15pm

    Optional group press photo

    You can attend without joining the photo. Read the photography guidance.

  3. 6.45pm

    Rally ends

    Leave entrances and pedestrian routes clear as people arrive for Council.

Planned rally times follow the parent rally flyer. Full Council is listed for 7pm, with York House Council Chamber as the proposed venue. That listing does not establish that the school proposal will be discussed or decided that evening.

Coming along

  • Dress for an outdoor evening and bring a weatherproof layer. A hand-held placard is welcome; keep the message respectful.
  • Children should stay with their parent or carer. Adults remain responsible for the children they bring.
  • Keep pavements, crossings, entrances, accessible routes and emergency access clear. This is planned as a stationary gathering, with no march.
  • If practical, use public transport. Plan your journey with TfL and check services before you travel.

Access and facilities are still being checked. We cannot yet confirm the step-free route, accessible gathering area or toilet availability. Contact Yann about what you need before making your journey.

Photos are your choice

The organised group press photo at 6.15pm is optional. If you want to stay out of it, do not join the photo group and tell the person arranging the picture.

Ask before taking or sharing an identifiable photo of another person. For children, get their parent or carer’s agreement and respect the child’s own willingness. Attending the rally does not give permission to publish someone’s image.

Independent press or other attendees may take photographs; organisers cannot promise that everyone will stay out of all images. If that matters to you, discuss it with Yann before attending.

Make your view part of the consultation

Coming to the rally does not submit a consultation response. Respond to the council by 16 October 2026 in your own words; you can respond now.
