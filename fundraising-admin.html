For the charity account administrator or treasurer · 25 September 2026

Help check the charity account setup

Proposal for review - charity approval pending. Donations are not open.

Yann Stoneman, a parent at Kew Riverside Primary School, is preparing a proposed fund through Donorbox and Stripe. Kew Riverside Primary School School Community (charity 1107444) is the proposed holder. Could you help check the account setup, or point us to its authorised owner?

Start with what already exists

Does the charity already use Donorbox or Stripe? Who has authority to run them and its bank account? A check of existing accounts can start now. Please do not send passwords, bank details or identity documents to Yann or in a reply.

1. Set up only with the right authority

Once the charity has approved the arrangement, its authorised person can accept provider terms and create or connect accounts. A Donorbox Admin connects the charity's Stripe through Payment Processor settings. Stripe must be active, with the required identity and bank checks complete. Admin access alone does not give trustee authority.

2. Check where money goes

Confirm the right charity and bank are connected, live payments and payouts work, and the actual fees and payout schedule are known. Donorbox does not hold the money pending rescue approval. Stripe payouts to the bank are separate from permission to spend. Keep this fund distinct in the accounts.

3. Check refunds before launch

Can the right person issue full, partial and bulk refunds for this account? Confirm limits for older payments and each payment method. How will money paid to the bank get back into the refund balance? Who covers fees that cannot be recovered? Check failed returns, closed cards and chargebacks too. A refund button still needs enough money.

4. Agree access and launch checks

Give helpers only the access trustees approve. Agree secure sign-in, backups and handover. Check receipts, the donor contact route, private records and reports. Keep monthly gifts and Gift Aid off until separately cleared. Use provider test tools where available; this brief does not authorise a live payment test or fundraiser launch.

What would help in your first reply

Please reply to the person who sent this brief with which accounts exist, who may administer them, any setup or refund gaps, and the next step you can arrange. We only need a status summary, not private account records.
