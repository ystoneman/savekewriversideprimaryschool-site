Our school. Our community. Our future.

Help save Kew Riverside Primary School

Richmond Council is consulting on a proposal to close Kew Riverside from 1 September 2027. No final decision has been made. Kew Riverside is a much-loved part of our community, and your voice can still shape what happens next.

Why it matters

A happy, inclusive and high‑achieving primary school

What happens next

Closure is proposed, not decided

This is the council’s planned timetable. Closure would only happen if the proposal is approved.

  1. Now

    16 October 2026

    Consultation closes. Last day to send your response.

  2. November 2026

    A council committee decides whether to move to formal consultation.

  3. April 2027

    The council plans to make a final decision.

  4. 1 September 2027

    Proposed closure date, only if the proposal is approved.

Source: council consultation leaflet, page 7. Full proposal & dates
