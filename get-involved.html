Help keep Kew Riverside Primary School open

Get involved

Closure is proposed, not decided. Here is what you can do to help keep Kew Riverside Primary School open.

Respond to the council

The council’s response form says responses must be submitted by 16 October 2026. Say how you are connected to the school, what you think and why, in your own words.

Respond to the council ↗︎

You can also email schoolorganisation@achievingforchildren.org.uk or write to School Organisation Proposals, First floor (east), Guildhall 2, High Street, Kingston upon Thames KT1 1EU (council leaflet, PDF page 8). Post may not arrive by 16 October; email is faster. Response checklist (PDF). A letter, video or petition does not replace your own official response.

The consultation response deadline has passed

The published response deadline, 16 October 2026, has passed. If the council approves the next stage in November 2026, it plans to publish statutory notices in January 2027, followed by a statutory representation period in January/February 2027. See the council’s timetable.

Sign the petition

Add your name to the petition on Richmond Council’s website. It is for people who live, work or study in Richmond borough, and a Richmond Account is required. It closes on 13 January 2027.

Sign the petition ↗︎

Write to your councillors and MP

The council’s timetable plans a committee decision in November 2026 on whether to move to statutory consultation. Explain why this matters to you and what you want them to do, in your own words. Find your Richmond councillors or find your MP.

Share your story

Write a community letter about what the school means to you, or read letters from other families. To help new families discover the school, parents can also share a short video testimonial for Kew Parent Voices on YouTube (adults only; keep children off camera).

Offer an idea or help

Send an idea, a question or an offer of help privately, or email savekrps@gmail.com.

Spread the word

Share this website with friends and neighbours. Making a flyer or poster? Download a QR code for the homepage: Homepage QR code, PNG image or Homepage QR code, SVG. Please publish flyers in your own name, and leave out community letters, videos and children’s details.

Making an Open Day flyer? Use the visit QR code instead: Visit QR code, PNG image or Visit QR code, SVG. It passes through this website straight to the school’s Open Day booking form, without showing campaign pages. Keep closure campaigning off Open Day flyers, and agree the wording with the school or PTA if you use their branding.
