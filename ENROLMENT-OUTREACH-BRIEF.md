# Proposed school-awareness pilot

Prepared 22 September 2026. Maintenance brief, excluded from the published website. This is a proposal for coordination; no school endorsement, partner agreement, assigned volunteer, approved spend or campaign launch is implied.

## Purpose and boundaries

Help prospective families understand Kew Riverside and enquire about a visit. The school and official admissions service handle availability, applications and family details. Keep the proposed closure and uncertainty visible alongside the school’s strengths. Additional enquiries or enrolments do not establish financial viability or a number of pupils that would prevent closure.

Use the existing [prospective-family destination](https://savekewriverside.org/index.html#visit-school), its optional [learning/results explanation](https://savekewriverside.org/understand.html#learning-and-results), the [school’s guided-tour enquiry](https://www.kewriverside.richmond.sch.uk/page/?pid=525&title=Contact+Us), and [Richmond’s official admissions route](https://www.richmond.gov.uk/primary_school_admissions). School contact and council landing page checked 22 September 2026. The school’s older open-day form ends on 7 January 2026; it is unsuitable for a new pilot until updated. The school admissions page’s older year heading is not current admissions guidance.

## Before anyone launches it

Agree a small pilot’s dates, visitor capacity, current places by year group, coordinator, channel owners, materials, costs and school review. Ask what recruitment has already been tried. Partners must agree to participate before they are described as partners. No outreach, posting or contact has been carried out as part of implementing the website plan.

Use the July inspection’s actual categories, including attendance and teaching improvements, and the dated whole-cohort attainment summary. Keep school statements, inspection observations, research and personal experiences distinct. Avoid promised outcomes, rankings or claims that mixed-age grouping caused the results. The school’s current curriculum map supplies an alternating-topic example; its conflicting class-count wording remains unresolved.

Parent Voices currently asks separately about YouTube publication. That permission does not cover reuse on other social platforms, advertising, nursery materials or publicity. Written contributions remain welcome. Use the existing website contribution/privacy routes; do not create a duplicate collection or repurpose private submissions.

## Measurement proposal

With the school’s agreement, report aggregate counts for a defined period: unique enquiries, completed visits, submitted applications and confirmed enrolments. Agree definitions, deduplication and the time lag between stages before comparing periods. Report costs and available capacity beside outcomes. Attribution is approximate; a voluntary source question may help but is not proof that a channel caused an enrolment. Do not invent targets or add website tracking to count clicks.

Keep identifiable family details with the school/admissions service. Do not request pupil records for campaign measurement. Agree safe aggregate reporting so small cells cannot identify individual families. Evaluate any sustained increase against a school/council recovery budget including staffing and funding timing; reach or applications alone do not demonstrate recovery.

## Review and decision

Campaign/community, UX/discovery and evidence reviewers examined the proposal. Their planning recommendations are reflected here: preserve direct enquiry and official admissions, broaden channels, retain consent boundaries, use aggregate stages, and hold unverified promises. An implementation-stage review and delivery checks are recorded separately in `CHANGELOG.md`.
