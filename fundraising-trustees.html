For the trustees · 25 September 2026

Could the charity host this fund?

Proposal for review - charity approval pending. Donations are not open.

Yann Stoneman, a parent at Kew Riverside Primary School, is preparing a rescue fund. He can draft the appeal and help with the page. Could the trustees review whether Kew Riverside Primary School School Community (charity 1107444) can host it?

The proposal

Raise money towards a viable plan to keep the school open for a defined period. Printing, advertising and legal advice would be funded separately. Neither a fundraising target nor spending can guarantee the school's future.

1. Can the charity accept these terms?

Please check the charity's current rules, legal form and powers. Before taking donations, we need approved terms covering the purpose, permitted costs, spending conditions, fees, returns, changes and surplus. This brief is not the full donor terms or an approval form.

2. When could money be spent?

The proposed tests are: a costed plan agreed with the school and relevant council decision-makers; the minimum money needed is available; and the charity's authorised decision-makers approve spending within its purposes and donor terms. Stripe may pay the charity's bank before then. A payout would not permit spending.

3. Can we keep money ready for returns?

Please agree what triggers a return if rescue cannot proceed, how fees are covered or deducted, and how unused money and surplus are handled lawfully. Money properly spent could not later be refunded; only the available balance could be returned. We need enough accessible cash and a workable refund method.

4. Who will decide, and on what timetable?

Who can arrange a valid trustee decision, approve payments, run accounts and refunds, and provide monthly updates? We prefer an ongoing appeal without all-or-nothing. The Charity Commission asks appeals to state a time limit, so the appeal duration and review/stop rules still need a decision.

What would help in your first reply

Please reply to the person who sent this brief with the right trustee contact, whether you can review this proposal, any concerns, and who can arrange the next discussion. An initial reply is not fund approval.
