# Kew Riverside evidence and editorial review

Check that a reader can distinguish established facts, attributed accounts, personal experience, editorial analysis and unresolved questions, and can reach the evidence behind a claim.

Read [the shared review workflow](../../../AGENTS.md#specialist-reviews) and [the current journey register](../../../UX-DESIGN-DECISIONS.md#protected-visitor-journeys). Resolve paths from this skill directory in the active checkout. Inspect the exact proposed wording/diff and its supporting sources. Use relevant entries in `sources.json`, the research data/source register and maintained pages; read the original source before certifying a claim. Verify volatile dates, roles, official process and provider details against current primary sources. If access fails, state what remains unverified.

## Domain checks

- Keep this parent's advocacy distinct from an official school, PTA, council or all-parent position. Source inclusion does not imply endorsement; holding an office does not establish a person's view or vote. Attribute personal accounts without presenting them as general scientific findings.
- Distinguish a proposal, consultation, conditional later stage and actual decision. Check the current official response route and date at implementation time. Do not treat a meeting listing as an agenda item, a planned date as an accomplished event or a site submission as an official response.
- Keep pupil roll, admissions, capacity, available places and forecasts distinct. Match year, geography, cohort and definition before comparing schools. A cumulative projected deficit is not an annual loss, a fundraising target or proof of the amount that would prevent closure; gross and avoidable costs differ.
- For attainment, distinguish subjects, combined measures, expected/higher standard, progress and ranking. Show cohort sizes and missing/suppressed values appropriately; do not replace missing with zero. Preserve changing trends and relevant newer evidence, including evidence inconvenient to the campaign. Do not compare a whole cohort with a selectively adjusted cohort.
- For inspections, distinguish the actual report, its hosting location, an index and a newsletter's account. Use the framework's actual category judgements and improvement areas; do not fabricate a single overall grade or publication date.
- For mixed-age provision, verify current Kew-specific curriculum/class details. Separate grouping, class size and structured tutoring; association or an inspector's observation does not establish causation. Keep international findings, counterevidence and limits in context, with no percentage reinterpretation of effect sizes.
- For other-schools research, distinguish the site's synthesis from original records; retain selection limits, school versus campaign/episode denominators, reprieve versus durable recovery, and closure comparisons. Cases illustrate possibilities, not a representative campaign success rate.
- Trace material corrections through all affected summaries, FAQ answers, timelines, cards, charts, tables, data and exports. Keep stable IDs and source/library counts consistent. Resolved gaps should lead visibly to the retrieved evidence instead of silently disappearing.

## Deliver the review

For each material claim at issue, identify the exact wording/location, primary source and relevant passage/page, what the evidence supports, and the smallest correction or qualification. Use the shared finding format and journey IDs. Distinguish a verified error from missing evidence or an access limitation. Hold only unsupported claims or missing data; do not block unrelated verified work.

Check that readers can discover the source and understand limitations where they encounter the claim. Reference current consent/privacy policy when the wording concerns publication; do not invent new permissions. Stay read-only and within the assigned scope: no contacting sources, publishing, submitting forms, editing files or spawning more reviewers. Do not present the review as professional legal advice or an independent human audit.
