# Kew Riverside campaign and community review

Assess whether the proposed or implemented change helps this parent-led school campaign turn interest into useful, voluntary action within its actual capacity.

Read [the shared review workflow](../../../AGENTS.md#specialist-reviews) and [the current journey register](../../../UX-DESIGN-DECISIONS.md#protected-visitor-journeys). Resolve these paths from this skill directory in the active checkout. Use the request and relevant current page/diff; consult the latest Parent action plan and official process sources when stage or timing matters. Do not load every historical plan by default or treat old proposals as implemented.

## Review questions

- Identify the visitor, the concrete action, the intended campaign outcome and who would receive or use the result. Distinguish formal participation, community testimony, evidence gathering, organising capacity and school recruitment. A new button or a view count is not an outcome.
- Preserve J2–J5's strong entry points. Fit additional asks into the relevant existing journey. Explain any competition with the official response, Parent action plan, Letters or Share ideas; do not demote practical family information to make campaigning more prominent.
- Match the request to realistic effort and volunteer capacity. An occasional contribution should have value in its own right; a larger responsibility needs a bounded task, support and an owner. Do not invent a volunteer, partnership, school endorsement or promised follow-up.
- Keep personal experience in the contributor's voice. Participation is voluntary and private options matter. Do not turn an evidence/question route into a pledge, public endorsement or campaign mailing list. Check the current contribution policy when suggesting reuse of letters or videos.
- Treat prospective families as people making a consequential choice: preserve a direct school visit/enquiry route, honest proposal context and useful admissions information. Additional campaign reading or contributing must not be required to enquire.
- Tie outreach to school-coordinated enquiries, visits, applications and enrolments where those can actually be observed. Separate measured stages and attribution from assumptions. Treat funding ideas as exploratory until a costed plan, recipient and authorisation exist.
- Check whether the next action fits the verified current process. Retire expired invitations while preserving historical records and shared links. Read current dates from maintained sources, not this skill.
- Protect relationships across the community, including families considering other schools. Flag unsupported disparagement, implied unanimity or claims that turnout/signatures alone determine the decision.

## Deliver the review

Use the shared finding format and relevant journey IDs. Recommend the smallest adjustment to the ask, placement, ownership or follow-through that resolves a concrete problem. Separate campaign hypotheses from observed evidence. If the actual blocker is a source claim or interface behaviour, identify it precisely for the lead to route to the other lens; do not spawn another reviewer.

Review only. Do not contact the school, parents or officials, launch outreach, collect pledges, submit forms, change permissions or publish content. The lead handles implementation within the user's existing scope.
