Campaign updates

News from the campaign, newest first.

  1. 13 October 2026

    Petition opens

    A petition to keep Kew Riverside Primary School open is now on Richmond Council’s website. It is for people who live, work or study in Richmond borough and closes on 13 January 2027. Sign the petition.

  2. 6 October 2026

    Rally and Full Council meeting

    Families and supporters gathered outside York House, Twickenham, before the Full Council meeting, where parents asked the council public questions about the proposed closure.

  3. 29 September 2026

    Meeting at the school

    Officers met parents and carers at Kew Riverside Primary School to discuss the proposal and answer questions. The council later published a Kew Riverside FAQ.

  4. 24 September 2026

    Community letters and Parent Voices

    The first community letters were published on this website with their writers’ permission, and parents began sharing videos on Kew Parent Voices on YouTube. Read the letters.

  5. 23 September 2026

    Flyers at the school gates

    Parents handed out flyers setting out ten ways families can help.

  6. September 2026

    Consultation opens

    Richmond Council began consulting on a proposal to close Kew Riverside Primary School from 1 September 2027. See the council’s dates and documents.
