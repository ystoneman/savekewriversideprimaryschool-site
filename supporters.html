    How names are confirmed and removed

    Consent. Confirmation.
    Human review.

    1. Your request stays private. It enters Yann’s review inbox.
    2. Yann confirms the details with you. The exact public name and statement need your reply before publication. This does not verify your legal identity.
    3. A person approves the entry. No AI or hourly task can automatically publish supporter names.

    There is no guaranteed review time. This permission covers the supporter statement only. It does not cover a testimonial, a council submission, signing up to a mailing list or registering as a volunteer.
