A clear next step for our school

Parents have questions about the closure process and how alternatives and children’s needs have been considered. Independent legal advice will help us understand our options.

This fund will help an education law firm review the process and advise on next steps. The school’s closure is proposed, not decided.

Knowing where your money goes

This parent-organised fund supports independent legal advice. Funding progress is updated manually.

This is a parent-organised fund, not a registered charity. Gift Aid is not available.
